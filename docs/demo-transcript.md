# Canary demo transcript

English narration by Sarvam Bulbul v3 using the Ishita voice.

## 00:00 · Opening

What if the first signs of a health event were already there, scattered across different systems? A dust spike, more clinic visits, and a change in sick leave can tell a different story when teams see them together. Canary brings those signals into one early warning workspace for population and workforce health.

## 00:19 · Sense

Start in the surveillance room. The Dubai map shows illustrative monitoring areas and their current risk tiers. Select an area to see the main signal, data completeness, and a suggested response. The wider surveillance map lets teams narrow their view to a district and inspect the monitoring units available to their demo role.

## 00:40 · Replay

Now replay a simulated respiratory episode. Scrub the timeline as environmental exposure and aggregate outcomes change. The different detector tracks show when each method would flag the event. The same workspace also supports gastrointestinal and heat stress scenarios. The scenarios are simulated so teams can inspect the decisions without using personal health records.

## 01:03 · Explain

Open an alert to see what changed. Compare observations with the expected baseline, then inspect the evidence contribution chart to see what drove the tier. The alert describes the area and time window and shows how complete the feeds were. When the evidence is incomplete, that uncertainty belongs in the decision.

## 01:23 · Act

An alert leads to a playbook. The reviewer can see the proposed action, who should review it, the evidence behind it, and the assumptions that matter. Canary supports a human review. The impact estimator lets teams explore how uptake and intervention effectiveness change the projected outcome, using assumptions they can adjust.

## 01:43 · Protect

Privacy is part of the workflow. The ingest sandbox accepts aggregate records and rejects person-level identifiers. In the suppression demo, change the threshold to see how small counts and related totals are hidden. The role selector shows how the same workspace changes with the reviewer’s scope.

## 02:02 · Validate

How well does it work? Across one hundred and forty-four injected events on held-out simulation seeds, Canary detected eighty-eight point nine percent, with ninety-two point three percent alert precision. It had fewer false alerts than clinic CUSUM, while clinic CUSUM detected more events. The validation screen shows the full tradeoff, including what happens when a feed drops out.

## 02:27 · Close

The next step is a shadow-mode pilot. Run Canary alongside existing processes, agree on an alert budget, and measure whether the earlier signals help people make better decisions. Until then, this is a demonstration for decision support. Explore the scenarios, review the evidence, and see where a small signal can change what you investigate.
